# research [company]

A lighter version of `prep` for early exploration: a quick brief on culture, interview reputation, and fit. Use it when the user is deciding where to apply or wants a read on fit before investing in full prep.

## Steps

1. If no company is given, ask for it (one question). Ask for the role/level being considered only if not in `coaching_state.md`.
2. Research using web search/fetch if available. Prioritize: the company's own careers/about/values pages, recent news, engineering/product/design blogs, public interview-experience write-ups, and reputable reviews. Follow sourcing rules in `reference/principles.md`: label **Verified / Inferred / Unknown** and give confidence. If you cannot browse, say so and offer to work from pasted material.
3. Read `coaching_state.md` for the user's background, target role and story bank.
4. Produce the brief (keep to about one page):

```
COMPANY BRIEF: <Company>   (Role considered: <role>)

1. What they do and where they are (3-4 lines)           [labels]
2. Culture signals (what they say vs. what evidence shows) [labels]
3. Interview reputation: typical stages, formats, what they screen for  [labels, confidence]
4. What they typically look for in <role>
5. Fit read: how your background maps
   - Strong matches:
   - Gaps / risks:
   - Overall: Pursue / Pursue with prep on X / Think twice, and why
6. Open questions worth answering before you apply (2-4)
```

5. Be candid about fit. Do not oversell the company or the user. If evidence on interviews is thin, say so.
6. Save to the Company block in `coaching_state.md` (status `researching`, brief, fit read).
7. Suggest the next step: `prep [company]` if they proceed, or `research [other company]` to compare.
