# debrief

Structured debrief after a rejection, so the user learns something from every no.

## Flow (one question at a time)

1. "What happened?" (company, role, round it ended at, how they found out, what feedback if any was given). Be warm but not saccharine; follow the directness level in delivery.
2. Ask how they **think** it went before the rejection and what they believe the reason was. Record it (self-assessment accuracy).
3. If a transcript or past analysis exists, use it. Otherwise ask for the 2-3 answers they remember being weakest and strongest.
4. **Extract learning**, separating:
   - **Within control:** answers, stories, preparation, communication patterns. Link to rubric dimensions and root causes (`reference/root_causes.md`) with evidence.
   - **Outside control:** headcount changes, internal candidates, hiring bar shifts, timing, stage-specific fit. Do not pretend you can know the reason; state plausible explanations as **inferred** and mark confidence Low unless the company said something.
   - **Unknown:** what you cannot tell.
5. **Proof points to reinforce:** which strengths were demonstrated and should be kept, and which claims need stronger evidence next time.
6. **Questions to ask the recruiter** (a short, professional, optional feedback request). Provide a drafted message of 60-100 words. Guidance: ask for specific, actionable feedback, express appreciation, and keep the door open; do not argue the decision. Note that many companies will not give detailed feedback.
7. **Carry-forward plan:** 2-3 concrete actions (e.g., a story to strengthen, a drill to run, a company-specific gap to research) with the exact commands.
8. Emotional check: briefly acknowledge the disappointment without therapizing, and do not catastrophize or minimize. If the user shows signs of serious distress, respond with care and suggest reaching out to someone they trust or a professional.

## Save

Company status → `rejected` with learning summary; Patterns updated; Open Items with the carry-forward actions; Session Log entry `debrief`. Next command: `practice [drill]` or `stories`.
