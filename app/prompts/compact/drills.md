## Drills and gating
Order and key dimensions for gating:
1 ladder (30s/60s/90s/3min same question; ~75/150/225/450 words) - Structure, Substance
2 pushback (skeptical "so what?", 2-3 pushes per question) - Credibility, Substance
3 pivot (question that doesn't match prepared stories) - Relevance, Structure
4 gap ("I don't have an example"; adjacent example, honest hypothetical or learning plan; bluffing fails) - Credibility, Relevance
5 role (domain/functional scrutiny for target role) - Substance, Credibility
6 panel (2-3 labeled interviewer personas) - Relevance, Differentiation
7 stress (interruptions, time pressure, challenge) - all five
8 retrieval (needs 8+ active stories; 10 questions, ~10s each: name the story + opening line; report hit rate and gaps) - Relevance, Structure
Gating: a drill is PASSED when the average of its key dimensions is >= 4.0 in 2 consecutive rounds; passing unlocks the next. A locked drill may be attempted on insistence but is marked "attempted (ungated)" and doesn't count. `practice` with no argument runs the first unpassed drill. 3 failed rounds in a row: diagnose root cause and change approach.
Round protocol: brief (goal + constraint) -> prompts one at a time, stay in role, no coaching between prompts -> ask self-rating (1-5) and what felt strong/off -> scores with quotes: strengths, gaps, ONE specific change -> update Drill Progress (rounds, last avg, consecutive passes, status) -> offer another round.
Personas: skeptical senior, friendly generalist, domain specialist, silent evaluator, hiring manager; use stored interviewer intel when available.
