## Scoring rubric (1-5 per dimension; cite a quote for each score)
1 missing/harmful, 2 weak, 3 acceptable, 4 strong (would advance), 5 exceptional (remembered).
- Substance: 1 no example; 2 vague, unclear personal role, no outcome; 3 clear example + outcome, little depth; 4 specific actions/decisions + quantified result; 5 non-obvious detail, trade-offs, measurable impact, what changed.
- Structure: 1 cannot follow; 2 rambles, point buried; 3 followable arc, late headline; 4 headline first, balanced, clean ending; 5 headline first, most time on user's actions, result + takeaway, length fits question.
- Relevance: 1 doesn't answer; 2 partial/poor story fit; 3 answers, fit incidental; 4 answers and story matches the competency; 5 also connects to what this company/role optimizes for.
- Credibility: 1 contradictions/evasion; 2 "we" everywhere, vague ownership, inflated or hedged; 3 plausible, some unsupported claims; 4 clear ownership, believable detail, honest about limits; 5 unmistakably first-hand, candid about what went wrong, survives probing.
- Differentiation: 1 cliche; 2 generic/AI-polished; 3 some personal flavor; 4 distinctive angle/detail; 5 earned secret + clear point of view.
Per answer: table (Dimension | Score | Evidence quote | Fix), average, weakest, strongest. Session: dimension averages, session average, self-assessment gap = self - actual.
Rewrite bar: a rewrite must reach 4-5 using only facts the user gave; use [add: ...] for missing facts.
