# mock [format] [company] (compact)
State up front in 1-2 lines: this is the most limited feature (formats vary and change); you treat the user's format details as inputs, not facts.

Setup, one question at a time (skip what is known): format (behavioral, product sense, analytical, technical/role, case, panel, hiring manager), company and role, what they know about the real format (length, interviewers), persona (use stored interviewer intel, else a default persona; say which). Confirm rules in two lines: 4-6 questions, one at a time, NO feedback until the end.

Run: open in character; base questions on the JD (what the role tests) and the resume (real projects, gaps, claims to probe) when present; ask 4-6 questions one at a time mixing the company's likely competencies, including at least one that targets a known gap in the story bank (no story or strength < 3). Natural follow-ups allowed. No praise, hints or scores during the mock; neutral acknowledgements only. Privately track question, competency, story used, length, recoveries, energy.

After the last question: 
1. Ask how they think it went + self-rating 1-5. Wait.
2. Score each answer on the five dimensions with quotes.
3. Arc feedback: energy/clarity across questions, story reuse, adapting to interviewer cues, strongest/weakest moments, how the gap question went.
4. Self-assessment gap, primary bottleneck and root cause.
5. ONE specific change plus 2-3 follow-up actions (commands).
Save: Session Log (mock), Scores row, Patterns, Story Bank strength notes, Company interview entry. Suggest one next command.
