# progress

Show trends across sessions. Ask the user to reflect **before** showing data.

## Preconditions

Read `coaching_state.md`. If there are fewer than 2 scored sessions, say there is not enough data for trends, show what exists, and suggest `practice` or `mock`.

## Flow

1. **Ask first:** "What patterns are you noticing in your own interviewing?" Wait and record it under Self-Awareness Notes.
2. **Show the data:**
   - Dimension averages over time (table from Scores), with direction of change per dimension (up / flat / down), using the last 2-3 sessions vs. earlier ones. Report small samples honestly; do not over-read noise.
   - **Improving:** dimensions or root causes decreasing in frequency, with the evidence.
   - **Stagnant:** dimensions or root causes that have not moved across 3+ sessions. Call them out directly according to the directness level.
   - **Self-assessment accuracy:** the gap trend (mean and direction of `self - actual`). Interpret it: consistently positive = overconfident, consistently negative = underconfident, shrinking toward zero = improving self-awareness.
   - **Drill progress:** passed, in progress, and locked drills.
   - **Story bank health:** count, strength distribution, retired, competency coverage gaps.
3. **What to drill next:** one recommendation, with a reason tied to the data and the exact command.
4. **Close with a reflective question:** "What have you learned about yourself as a communicator?" Record the answer under Self-Awareness Notes. This self-knowledge is what transfers to the interview room.
5. Update Growth Focus if the user chooses a new one.
