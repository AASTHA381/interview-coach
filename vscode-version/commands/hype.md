# hype

A pre-interview confidence kit to use right before walking in.

## Inputs

Read `coaching_state.md`: the upcoming Company block, stories, concerns, interviewer intel, and metrics. Ask one question only if the upcoming interview is unclear.

## Output (four parts; keep it tight so it can be used on the day)

### 1. 60-second hype reel
Biggest wins and strongest metrics, formatted to **read aloud** (about 150 words, short punchy lines). Use only real facts from the story bank and profile. Mark `[need metric]` rather than inventing numbers.

### 2. The 3x3 sheet
- **3 likely concerns** with a one-line counter each (from `concerns` data; generate if missing).
- **3 questions to ask** (from `questions` data; generate if missing).
If interviewer intel exists, reference the interviewer's likely focus area.

### 3. Ten-minute physical and mental warm-up
A timed routine, general wellness only (no medical claims): breathing (e.g., slow exhale-focused breathing), posture and voice warm-up, a quick read-aloud of the hype reel, opening-line rehearsal for the 2-3 most likely stories, a short visualization of the first two minutes, and a final check (water, tech, notes).

### 4. Mid-interview recovery scripts
Short lines the user can say, written in plain, natural language:
- **When you bomb an answer:** pause, own it, and restart ("Let me take another run at that. The short version is...").
- **When you get a question with no story:** buy time, be honest, and bridge to the closest true example or reason out loud ("I haven't faced exactly that, but the closest thing was...").
- **When you blank:** a 5-second reset ("Give me a moment to collect my thoughts").
- **When you ramble:** a clean landing ("To bring that back to your question...").
- **When the interviewer pushes back:** hold position calmly, concede only what is true.

Never include scripts that involve lying. Finish with one line of encouragement that is specific and evidence-based (e.g., a real strength from their record), not generic. Next command: `thankyou` after the interview.
