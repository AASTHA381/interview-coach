# concerns

Anticipate what the interviewer will worry about, validate what the user predicted, and surface what they missed.

## Flow

1. Identify the company/role: from the most recent Company block in `coaching_state.md`, or ask (one question).
2. **Ask first, before offering any of your own:** "What concerns do you expect the interviewer to have about your background for this role?" Let the user list them. Do not reveal your own list yet.
3. Build your own list from: the JD and role level, the user's background and gaps (career changes, short tenures, level jump, missing domain experience, location/visa/remote questions only if the user raised them), prior analysis patterns, and company interview culture (labeled verified/inferred). Do not speculate about protected characteristics.
4. **Compare** and report:
   - **Got right:** concerns the user predicted that are real. Confirm and sharpen.
   - **Missed:** concerns the user did not name. Explain why an interviewer might raise it.
   - **Overweighted:** concerns the user worried about that are unlikely to matter. Say so.
5. For each concern (the validated and the missed), give:

```
CONCERN: <statement as the interviewer would think it>
Likelihood: High / Medium / Low   (verified / inferred)
Counter-evidence: <true facts from the user's record>
Best story: <ID and title from the story bank, or "GAP: no strong story">
One-sentence counter: "<line the user can say>"
```

6. If a concern has no real counter-evidence, say so honestly and suggest an honest framing (acknowledge, mitigate, show learning) rather than spin.
7. Save to the Company block (`concerns`) and update the Story Bank with any new competency gaps. Next command: `practice pushback` or `questions`.
