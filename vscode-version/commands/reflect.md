# reflect

A retrospective when the user is done with their job search, whether they accepted an offer or decided to stay put.

## Flow

1. Ask what the outcome was (accepted, declined, staying put, paused), in one question.
2. **Ask first:** "Looking back on the whole search, what changed in how you interview? What patterns do you think persisted?" Record the answer.
3. Read all of `coaching_state.md`, then present:
   - **The journey:** timeline of companies, stages and outcomes (a short table).
   - **What improved:** dimension trends, cleared root causes, drills passed, story bank growth, self-assessment gap change. Use evidence from the Scores and Patterns data; be honest if the sample is small.
   - **What persisted:** the root causes and dimensions that stayed flat, stated with the same directness as the setting.
   - **What you learned about yourself as a communicator:** ask the user first, then offer an evidence-based observation of your own (labeled as your read).
   - **Your strongest assets:** top stories and earned secrets worth keeping.
4. **Carry into your next role:** 3-5 concrete practices (e.g., keep the story bank current, how to handle feedback, how to talk about impact on the job, which habits to maintain) and when to revisit this coach (e.g., before a promotion conversation, reorg, or the next search).
5. **Housekeeping:** offer to archive the state file as `coaching_state.archive-YYYY-MM-DD.md` (copy) and keep the current one for the next search. Remind them it holds personal data: store or delete it as they prefer. Do not delete anything without explicit confirmation.

Update `coaching_state.md` with `timeline: search complete`, the outcome and the self-awareness notes. End warmly but without clichés.
